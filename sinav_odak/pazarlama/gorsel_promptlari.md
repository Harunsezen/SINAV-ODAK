# SINAV ODAK — 5 GÖRSEL PROMPTU (Instagram / TikTok)

**Estetik:** yüzsüz, sinematik, mühendis-öğrenci. Gece masası, tek sıcak
ışık, soğuk gölge. İnsandan yalnızca **el** görünür.

## Kampanya omurgası (beş görselde de sabit)

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Palet | indigo `#4F5BD5` · mola teal `#2FA3A0` · seri `#E9603A` · mürekkep `#232636` · kâğıt `#FCF9F2` |

## ÖNEMLİ — ekran yazısı

Görsel üreticiler Türkçe metni bozuyor (ş/ğ/ı kayboluyor, sahte kelime
üretiyor). Bu yüzden promptlar **ekranda okunabilir yazı istemiyor**:
telefon ekranı "büyük dairesel sayaç halkası + soyut yer tutucu bloklar"
olarak tarif ediliyor.

**Gerçek arayüz sonradan bindirilecek.** Uygulamanın kendi ekran
görüntüleri hazır: `qa_shots/`, `qa_devices/`, `qa_book/`. İş akışı:
görseli üret → telefon ekranını Photoshop/Figma'da perspektifle
değiştir. Böylece hem sinematik ışık hem gerçek arayüz elde edilir.

## 1 — "SAYAÇ BAŞLADI" (kampanya açılış görseli)

**Amaç:** ürünün tek cümlesi — başla, bitir. Feed'in ilk karesi.

```
Cinematic overhead three-quarter still life of a student's night desk,
shot from a 45-degree high angle. A modern smartphone lies flat on dark
oak, screen glowing bright: a large circular progress ring in deep indigo
(#4F5BD5) around a bold placeholder numeral, with three small abstract
blocks below it — clean minimal UI, no legible text. A young person's
hand enters from the lower right, index finger just about to touch the
screen, fingertip barely 1 cm above the glass, motion suspended. Around
the phone: an open hardcover mathematics textbook with dense diagrams,
a mechanical pencil resting in the gutter, a half-drunk black coffee in
a plain white ceramic cup, a folded pair of wired earbuds.

Lighting: single warm practical from an articulated metal desk lamp at
upper right, 3000K, creating a tight pool of light on the book and a long
soft shadow to the lower left; the rest of the room falls into cool
blue-grey darkness (#232636). The phone screen is the second light
source, casting indigo bounce onto the fingers and the coffee cup rim.

Shot on a 50mm prime at f/2.0, shallow depth of field, focus on the
fingertip and the screen ring, textbook edges softly falling off. Fine
film grain, subtle chromatic aberration, no bloom. Muted, desaturated
palette except the indigo screen glow. Photorealistic editorial product
photography, dark academia mood, 4:5 aspect ratio.
```

**Negatif:** `face, person, portrait, body, smiling, text, letters, words,
logo, watermark, cartoon, illustration, 3d render, oversaturated,
lens flare, cluttered desk, plastic look`

**Alt yazı önerisi (TR):** "Tahmin etmeyi bırak. Sayacı başlat."

## 2 — "01:12" (atmosfer / gece)

**Amaç:** gece çalışan öğrencinin kendini gördüğü kare. Ürün değil, an.

```
Cinematic low-angle macro still of a smartphone standing upright in a
minimal desk stand, photographed almost at desk level so the phone
dominates the frame. The screen shows a very large circular timer ring,
roughly two-thirds depleted, deep indigo (#4F5BD5) on near-black, with a
bold placeholder numeral at its centre — no legible text, no icons.
Behind and beside it, thrown out of focus: an open textbook lying face
down, a cold black coffee with a dried ring on the ceramic, a crumpled
sheet of squared paper, the blurred bulb of a desk lamp burning as a
warm orange orb in the background bokeh.

The room is dark. Time reads late. The only illumination is the desk
lamp behind the subject (rim light, warm 2700K, edging the phone and the
cup in a thin gold line) and the phone's own indigo screen glow washing
forward over the desk grain toward the camera.

Shot on an 85mm macro at f/1.8, extremely shallow depth of field, focus
razor-thin on the timer ring, everything else dissolving into creamy
bokeh. Heavy atmospheric depth, visible dust motes in the lamp beam,
fine grain, deep true blacks, slight teal shift in the shadows.
Photorealistic, contemplative, lonely-but-determined mood.
4:5 aspect ratio.
```

**Negatif:** `face, person, hands, text, letters, numbers with serifs,
logo, watermark, bright daylight, white background, hdr, clean studio`

**Alt yazı önerisi (TR):** "Sayaç duraklatılamaz. Başladığın bloğu
bitirirsin."

## 3 — "KARNE" (fark yaratan özellik — veli/öğretmen)

**Amaç:** PDF karne. Tek gündüz kare; seriyi kasten kırıyor.

```
Cinematic overhead flat-lay of a wooden dining table in early morning
light. Three printed A4 sheets are fanned across the frame — clean
report-card layouts on warm cream paper (#FCF9F2) with a coloured header
band, a row of large rounded stat cards, and a simple bar chart; the
typography is deliberately soft and out of focus so no wording is
readable. Beside the sheets, a smartphone lies face up showing an
abstract statistics screen: coloured horizontal bars in indigo, teal and
amber on a light background. An adult hand and a teenager's hand are both
partially in frame at opposite edges, one holding a corner of a sheet,
the other resting near the phone — only hands and forearms, no bodies.
A cup of tea in a tulip glass, a pair of reading glasses folded on the
table, a set of car keys.

Lighting: soft directional morning daylight from a window at the left,
5200K, gentle and diffuse, long low-contrast shadows; a warm bounce from
the wooden table. Bright, calm, domestic — deliberately the opposite of
the night-desk frames.

Shot on a 35mm at f/2.8 from directly above, mild vignette, natural
colour, fine grain. Photorealistic editorial lifestyle photography,
honest and unstaged. 4:5 aspect ratio.
```

**Negatif:** `face, portrait, smiling, stock photo, text, readable words,
logo, watermark, office, corporate, cold blue light, clutter`

**Alt yazı önerisi (TR):** "Veliye ve öğretmene tek dokunuşla PDF. Veri
telefonda üretilir, hiçbir yere gitmez."

## 4 — "KİTAP OKUMA MODU" (v1.3 — yeni özellik)

**Amaç:** yeni modun tanıtımı. Ders değil, roman. Daha sıcak, daha yumuşak.

```
Cinematic close still life of a reading corner at night. A worn paperback
novel lies open, spine cracked, pages catching warm light, resting on a
dark green wool blanket over the arm of a fabric armchair. Propped
against the book, a smartphone shows a minimal screen: one very large
circular counter ring in teal (#2FA3A0) and a small horizontal progress
bar beneath it — no legible text. A hand rests loosely on the open page,
thumb holding the paper flat, fingers relaxed, no tension. Beside it a
glass cup of black tea on a small saucer, steam barely visible, and a
paper bookmark with a folded corner.

Lighting: a single warm floor lamp just out of frame at upper left,
2700K, soft and enveloping; the rest of the room is deep shadow. The
phone adds a cool teal accent that separates it from the amber book —
two temperatures, one frame.

Shot on a 50mm at f/1.8, shallow depth of field, focus on the phone
counter and the hand, page texture and blanket fibres visible.
Warm-cool colour contrast, gentle film grain, no harsh highlights.
Photorealistic, calm and unhurried, the opposite of exam pressure.
4:5 aspect ratio.
```

**Negatif:** `face, person, text, letters, logo, watermark, harsh light,
studio, cold sterile, textbook, exam paper, clutter`

**Alt yazı önerisi (TR):** "Roman da çalışmadır. Süre ve sayfa tutulur,
seri kırılmaz."

## 5 — "SERİ" (disiplin / streak)

**Amaç:** alışkanlık. En güçlü tutundurucu ve en paylaşılabilir kare.

```
Cinematic medium-close still of a wall above a study desk, shot slightly
from below. A grid of small square paper notes is pinned to a cork board;
most bear a single thick hand-drawn diagonal mark in orange marker, a few
at the end are still blank. A hand holding a chunky orange marker is
caught mid-stroke, adding the mark to the next empty square — the stroke
half-finished, ink glossy and wet. Below the board, on the desk edge and
partly out of focus, a smartphone leans against a stack of books, its
screen showing a single large flame-shaped glyph in ember orange
(#E9603A) beside a bold placeholder numeral on a dark background.

Lighting: a hard warm desk lamp from the lower right rakes across the
cork texture, giving the paper notes strong side-shadow and physical
relief; the upper corners of the frame fall to darkness. Ember orange is
the dominant accent, the rest of the palette pushed to grey-brown.

Shot on a 35mm at f/2.2, focus on the marker tip and the wet stroke, the
phone soft in the foreground bokeh. Tactile, textured, slightly gritty.
Fine grain, deep contrast, no gloss. Photorealistic documentary style.
4:5 aspect ratio.
```

**Negatif:** `face, person, portrait, text, letters, words, calendar
numbers, logo, watermark, neon, cyberpunk, illustration, clean minimal
studio`

**Alt yazı önerisi (TR):** "Zinciri kırma. Kitap okuduğun gün de sayılır."
